Cadence Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Data We Collect

Nothing. All data, settings, and library information stays entirely local on your device.

2. Music Files

Your music files are never uploaded, never transmitted, and never shared. Cadence plays them directly from your local file system.

3. Last.fm Integration

If you choose to enable Last.fm scrobbling, only the song title, artist, and album are sent to Last.fm to log your plays. This feature is entirely optional and under your control.

4. Discord Integration

If you choose to enable Discord Rich Presence, only the current song title and artist are sent to your local Discord client to display your status. This feature is entirely optional and under your control.

5. Lyrics

Lyrics are fetched from LRCLIB. Only the song title and artist are sent as a search query to retrieve the lyrics.

6. Cover Art

Cover art is fetched from iTunes/MusicBrainz when no local art is found. Only the song title and artist are sent as a search query.

7. Analytics

Cadence contains zero tracking, telemetry, or analytics. Your usage is your business.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact: your.email@example.com
